What makes it different

It tells you when it is wrong

Most tools show you the run that worked. KorisQuant AI reports the result it actually measured, including the unflattering ones.

  • Agents that lose are shown losing. If a strategy underperforms buy & hold after costs, the dashboard says so.
  • Simulated data is labelled. When a live provider is unreachable the synthetic fallback is badged, never passed off as real.
  • Intervals are validated. Conformal coverage is measured against outcomes rather than assumed from a normal curve.
  • No leakage. Train and test splits are strictly disjoint, enforced by an assertion at runtime.
Questions

Before you start

Is this investment advice?

No. KorisQuant AI is educational and research software. Every output is a statistical estimate produced by a model, and markets are non-stationary — past performance never guarantees future results. Validate independently before risking capital.

Do I need an account?

You can open the dashboard and explore immediately. An account keeps your portfolios and preferences across sessions.

Where does the market data come from?

Live quotes come from public providers, led by Yahoo Finance. If none responds, a deterministic synthetic engine takes over so the platform stays usable — and everything derived from it is badged SIMULATED.

Can the AI assistant make things up?

It has no market data in its prompt. To state any figure it has to call one of fifteen read-only tools that query the same engines the dashboard uses, and each answer lists the tools it consulted.
